# Day 06 – Advanced Excel Dashboard with Interactive Business Analytics

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Excel dashboard using the Superstore dataset to analyze sales, profit, customer, product, and regional performance.

## Tasks Completed
- Used the cleaned dataset stored in an Excel Table (`Table2`).
- Worked on KPI cards for business performance analysis.
- Created PivotTables and PivotCharts for business insights.
- Created a Top 10 Products by Sales PivotTable with a value filter.
- Created a Top 10 Customers by Sales PivotTable.
- Worked on Customer Segment slicer connections.
- Worked on dashboard formatting and visual consistency.
- Prepared a concise Executive Business Insights Report in PDF format.

## Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard focuses on:
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales Distribution by Category
- Top 10 Customers by Revenue

## Key Business Insights
- **Highest Revenue Region:** West
- **Best Performing Category:** Office Supplies
- **Highest Sales Month:** December 2023
- **Top Customer:** Sean Miller

## Challenges Faced
The main challenges included creating dynamic PivotTables, connecting slicers across charts, applying Top 10 filters, recovering a deleted worksheet using Version History, and maintaining consistent dashboard formatting.

## Business Recommendations
1. Focus on high-performing regions and customer segments.
2. Improve sales strategies for low-performing products.
3. Monitor monthly sales trends to identify growth opportunities.
4. Strengthen customer retention through targeted offers.
5. Regularly monitor profit margins and business performance.

- Business Insights Report (PDF)
